# 2026-10-06: an update from master is seeded from the mirror's index (batch 102)

From the person's question of why CI's Intel job takes most of an hour. It comes after v0.3.0-rc3.

## What changed

- **An update planned from master seeds its PortIndex from MacPorts' mirror.** The mirror bootstrap brackets the tree's commit against the mirror index's date, and re-indexes only what changed since; an update from master names that commit only as its source's base, and the selection reader drops bases (`Stager.WithoutBase`), so it had no commit and indexed the whole tree: 16 minutes in CI on Intel, about 3½ here. The Stager now keeps a base that is a commit of the very tree as the tree's commit, and `Stage` does the same for a source that is its own base. Here the plan went from 209 seconds to 19, re-indexing 23 changed paths.
- **CI clones three days of the ports tree's history** (8a28e948), so the bracket commit is there; alone it changed nothing, since no commit reached the bootstrap.
- **full.sh --end** logs dhtest's gh out once a run is over, since reset-user.sh keeps its login in plain text, and names the results to copy.

## Verification

- Tests: the mirror bootstrap seeds a source that names its commit only as its base, through `Stage` and through a Stager that drops bases; it fails without the fix.
- The local suite had been run with `/opt/local/libexec/macports/bin/tclsh8.6`, beside which there's no `portindex`, so every portindex test skipped; CI uses `/opt/local/bin/port-tclsh`, and so does this batch's run.
- The full checks: fmt-check, vendor-check, vet, lint, deadcode, and the suite under MacPorts.
