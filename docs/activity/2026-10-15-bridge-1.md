# Bridge 1 goes: clean --legacy and the pre-v3 branches (v0.4.0 part 1, hygiene)

Under the person's no-bridges rule, as the command package split's
coalescing placed it in hygiene (and PR #4 recommended), the bridge to
dockhand before v3 goes: `clean --legacy`, setup's "Old branches" line,
clean's count of them, engine/legacy.go, and usage.md's paragraph. Its
files are clear of PR #4, so it went ahead of the rest of hygiene.

First, the roadmap's one `clean --legacy` preview on the person's Mac, with
a v0.3.1 build (the database is schema 31): 8 branches from before v3,
codex's named for a look and 7 kept for `adopt`, none to remove. They stay
plain Git branches: `dockhand adopt` takes one up, `git branch -D` removes
one, and `setup --json` carries no legacy count.

- engine/legacy.go and its test go: `LegacyBranchNames`, `PlanLegacy`,
  `RemoveLegacy`, and the kinds. `portMoves` and `superseded`, which
  `clean --archived` also reads to name an archived branch master has
  moved past, move to clean.go.
- command's clean loses `--legacy`, its plan, view, and count line, and
  `legacy` in its JSON; "nothing to clean: --merged or --closed names
  what". setup loses its "Old branches" line.
- usage.md says what becomes of the pre-v3 branches, and keeps `clean
  --archived`'s look at a branch master moved past.

Tests: TestArchiveAsksCommitOrDiscard (`--legacy` is unknown; clean names
what to clean), with TestLegacyCleanupKeepsWhatHoldsAnotherCommit and
TestCleanSaysWhatItDoesWithBranchesFromBeforeV3 removed with what they
