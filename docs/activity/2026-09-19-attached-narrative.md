# One voice on an attached command

Since bump publishes by default and runs the driver itself, its terminal carried two voices: the reporter's line per state change, built from the job's recorded detail ("Pushed verified branch author/ports:dockhand/bump/jq at 8b7f…; checking the remote before publishing"), and the driver's own progress reports at info ("Tart capacity reserved; cloning image", "Starting verification VM and waiting for the guest agent"), followed by the summary saying it a third time.

The rule now is that who is reporting decides the level. An attached command narrates its job at info in the status table's words, one line per milestone and none repeated: the change once the release is resolved, the branch once prepared, "waiting for capacity" or "building on macOS 26", the verdict with its platform, "publishing", and the pull request with created or updated; a failure, needs-attention, or cancellation prints its state and detail, and a completion that the verdict and PR lines did not already say, such as an unverified publication or a branch-only job, prints its outcome. `-v` prints the previous stream, every state change with the job's detail, reuse decisions, and admission counts.

The driver's reports are demoted while a command is attached: `progress.Quiet` lowers info reports made under a context to verbose, and the process manager's `Attach` drives its cycles under a context derived by a new `Drive` hook, which the CLI sets to `Quiet`. `Run`, which `start` and the live status table use, drives under the plain context, so their terminals keep every provider and index line at info. Nothing in the engine or providers changed. The summary on stdout stays as the result; the narrative is on stderr, so a pipe sees one and a terminal sees both, which is the reports-versus-results split output.md chose.

Tests: the narrative for a bump from resolution to pull request, with a repeated snapshot adding no lines and the driver's detail absent; a failed verification's two lines; the quiet context's level change; and the manager driving attached cycles under the derived context while a resident run does not.
