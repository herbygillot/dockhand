# 2026-10-03: Tart's state beside dockhand's Tart home (batch 77)

Batch 77 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md): step 3, from the M1's first full quick stage at 07808ae3 (the Prime-time release bar thread), whose early finding was that `tart-images` and `tart-locks` leaked into the person's real `~/.dockhand`.

## What changed

- **dockhand's Tart locks and image records sit beside its Tart home.** Both went to `~/.dockhand/tart-locks` and `~/.dockhand/tart-images` whatever `DOCKHAND_TART_HOME` said, so the quick stage, which pins that home in its scratch directory, wrote into the person's own `~/.dockhand`. `tart.StateDirectory` is the directory dockhand's Tart home is in: `~/.dockhand` by default, as before, and the home's parent where `DOCKHAND_TART_HOME` places it elsewhere. The locks, the image records, and provisioning's import scratch (`imports`) are all there. Someone who set `DOCKHAND_TART_HOME` before loses setup's records of their images from view: those images read as having no recorded origin, as images made before records existed do, so an earlier result isn't matched to them by origin.
- The provisioning pin moved, since `connect.go` changed; what an image holds didn't, so `SetupProtocol` stays.
