# 2026-10-02: update and outdated, and the readers only fakes stood in for (batch 56)

Batch 56 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md): D19, field testing's update findings from its eighth and ninth reports and the retry of batch 52, and the test plan's step 3 for the readers.

## What changed

- **update --outdated says every port it's given.** A port already at its newest release said nothing, so pomo and tokei, named in `update --outdated pomo pgdog tokei`, went unsaid; they're said now, "Current: pomo 0.8.1, tokei 14.0.0", as is one that couldn't be checked, or whose tracked branch moved.
- **D19: a port with `cargo.update` on is taken** where its source ships a Cargo.lock, the person's word: its crates are regenerated from that lock, the update says "cargo.update is on; MacPorts re-resolves offline against these crates.", and dockhand never sets or clears the option. A source without a Cargo.lock is refused as what it is, "cargo.update is on and 1.0's source ships no Cargo.lock, so its crates can't be regenerated"; a value that's neither yes nor no is refused as before. A block's notices are one list, `Regenerated.Notices`, which pgdog's now joins.
- **go2port's refusal names what it fetched**: "go2port's output for codeberg.org/kevinschoon/pomo 0.8.2 does not cover the source go.mod requirements exactly; go2port fetches the module from codeberg.org itself", where it named nothing, and the regeneration's progress names the input's tag, where it said an empty one. Which hosts go2port fetches from is go2port's; pomo still needs `go.offline_build no` until it fetches from Codeberg.
- **A workspace's dependency is counted once for what happened to it**, where wasmtime's line named cranelift-isle twice. mise's "6 workspace members: 112 added" shrinks by its repeats, but a new member's dependency an old member already declared still counts as added: telling them apart needs the old members' manifests, which this count doesn't read.
- **The readers only fakes stood in for are tested** (step 3): `dependentsIn` against a written PortIndex, each dependent once with its phases and none from the changed directories; GitHub's `CommitTime` and `Describe`, and GitLab's `CommitTime`, against servers answering as each documents; create's GitHub project reader, its newest release that's neither a draft nor a prerelease, its files at that commit, and its refusals; and outdated's reading of discovery, and `unresolvedWords`.
- **Two wrappers only tests called are gone**, their tests pointed at the entry points dockhand uses: `depblock.Apply` for `Plan.ApplyPlain`, and `buildlog.First` for `FirstFrom`. One near-duplicate is folded: a target `--only` left out whose tag moved is a case of `TestASourceMovedSinceItsCheckIsAConcern`.
