# 2026-10-02: an assessment that's whole (batch 32)

Batch 32 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md), the first of the core made solid: the architecture review's finding 1, batch 22's two leftovers, and the scope's rule moved to its owner.

## What changed

- **One collector, and update's patches checked** (the [architecture review](../reviews/2026-10-01-architecture-and-helper-ownership.md)'s finding 1). Update and a revision's assessment each assembled `assess.Input`, and update's never checked patches, while its record was kept as the revision's whole assessment, so `submit` and `review` reused one with no patch results. Both now go through one collection step (`Engine.collect`): the candidate's Portfile read, the providers its requirements name observed, the rules applied. And one patch check (`patchesFor`, which was `revisionPatches`): update hands it the results its preparation already has, as they stand, and the archives it kept, so only the patches the candidate drops are checked anew. `assess.Policy` is 14, so a record kept without patches is made again. The review's probe is a regression test, its assertions turned.
- **A submission's concerns in its JSON** (batch 22's leftover). `submit --json` gives `concerns`, each with its origin, port, rule, subject, and class, for a person's own submission too; a moved tag, `release-moved` or `source-moved`, was said only in text and in an unattended submission's `held`.
- **An assessment of another commit than was built is a concern** (batch 22's other leftover). A Git-fetched port's assessment keeps the commit it read (`UpstreamComparison.Commit`); where the check a submission rests on planned another, as after a hand edit, which records no release for `release-moved` to compare, it's `assessment-moved`: "libharbor's assessment read aaaaaaa, and check-7 planned bbbbbbb: what upstream's change was judged of isn't the source the check built".
- **CI's rule is macports'** (the review's smaller items). `ScopeOf` and the Portfile-or-`files/` rule (`macports.ChangedPort`) moved beside `macports/layout.go`; the engine keeps `Scope` as an alias.
- **A changed directory's other ports are named** in `impact`, "Changed ports jq and jq-devel", and in `review`'s summary, as the base's index has them (`PortsDefined`), where devel/libuv read as libuv alone (the batch 11 run on #34620). `impact --json` gives them as `defined`. Where the index can't be read, nothing is said wrongly.
