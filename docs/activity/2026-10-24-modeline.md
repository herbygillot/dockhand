# 2026-10-24: An edit gives its Portfile MacPorts' modeline

The person's ask, at 04:41Z: dockhand adds or corrects the modeline on the
Portfiles it touches, by default, with a flag to keep it from doing so.
Prioritization placed it in 80d03b18, a fix for main riding the release
after v0.5.0.

## What's done

- **portfile.WithModeline** gives a Portfile MacPorts' modeline as line 1.
  `create`'s `Modeline` constant moved here, and portcreate refers to it.
  - It's added, with a blank line after it, where line 1 is no modeline.
  - It replaces a modeline whose settings are the same, spelled otherwise:
    `filetype=tcl`, a missing `mode: tcl`, or `vim: fenc`.
  - A modeline that sets anything else stays, since the file's indentation
    follows it: tabs, `noet`, a width other than 4, another mode or
    encoding.
- **Every edit `update` makes** gets it, after the preparer and any
  `--follow-moves`, with the prepared tree remade (`withModelines`). That
  covers bump, ship, serve, checksums, revbump and `--revbump-dependents`,
  which all go through it. Line 1 is a comment, which nothing evaluates.
- **`--keep-modeline`** on update, checksums, revbump, bump, ship and serve
  sets `engine.Engine.KeepModeline` for the command's whole run. serve
  passes it on to the agent `--install` makes.
- **Tests:** TestTheModelineIsAddedOrCorrected (portfile) covers the
  spellings, tabs, another width, and none. TestAnUpdateGivesItsPortfileTheModeline
  (engine) covers none, spelled otherwise, tabs, and `--keep-modeline`. Twelve
  tests that read a fixture Portfile written without a modeline now expect
  one. The corpus, 14 rows, is unchanged: 14 of 14 in 3 min 14 s.

## What isn't, and why: tidy

The roadmap's entry also has `tidy` give the modeline to the Portfiles it
commits. Built in tidy's working-file step, it broke two tests, for one
reason. tidy's commit then holds a tree that the working files, and any
check of them, don't:

- A check of the branch's uncommitted files no longer matches what tidy
  commits. TestARegroupPuttingADependentFirstIsNoted lost the check's order
  for its commits, and submit would ask for a new check.
- A saved plan (`tidy --plan --out`) reads as stale, "its files were
  edited", since its tree isn't the working files'.

So tidy is left as it was, and the question went back to prioritization. A
hand edit gets the line at the next edit dockhand makes of the port.
