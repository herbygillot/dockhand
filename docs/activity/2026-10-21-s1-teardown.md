# S1's teardown lets serve finish

Prime-time (2026-10-21): S1 uninstalled serve while it was checking, which
left check-448 (mysql8) stopped, with its Tart clone, cleared by cancel
and clean.

S1 now waits, a minute at a time, for no check to be queued or running
but stopped ones (`s1_idle`). It waits up to `ACCEPT_S1_DRAIN_MINUTES`,
120 by default. serve may queue more as it looks, so the wait is bounded.
Past it, the row says so in its samples. Then it uninstalls serve, and
`serve --drain` finishes whatever the uninstall stopped. The VMs are
listed after that, so "no orphaned VM" reads what serve left behind. A
`serve --drain` while the agent leads would only follow it, which is why
the drain comes after the uninstall. Harness only.
