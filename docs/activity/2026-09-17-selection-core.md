# Stage 2 of the resolution consolidation: one selection core

Automatic selection over forge catalogs and over livecheck listings each carried their own copy of the same steps: admit candidates by stability, ask MacPorts for the newest one through the port's livecheck expression, reject none or several, classify the result, and word the assessment. `upstream/candidates.go` now owns those steps: `admits` and `followsPrereleases` for admission, `newest` for the native choice and its two failure modes, and `Result.finish` for classification and assessment. The catalog and listing paths call them, so a policy change lands once. The messages the two paths print are unchanged except that "no eligible version" no longer says "stable", which was wrong for prerelease ports. Explicit resolution keeps its own path, since its input is a requested spelling rather than a candidate set, but shares the classification. No behavior changed.
