## What changed

- **A plan orders changed ports by what they depend on through unchanged ones.** With `--only py31x-safetensors,py31x-mlx-vlm`, the plan put mlx-vlm before safetensors: mlx-vlm depends on safetensors only by way of py-transformers, which the branch doesn't change, and the plan ordered by direct dependencies between its targets alone. It passed only because the guest resolved from the branch's tree and built safetensors 0.8.0 on the way. Where the port in between comes from a published archive, the order is what makes the build use the branch's. Where a plan has two candidates or more, it now reads every port's direct dependencies from the revision's port index (`portindex.Index.Dependencies`), follows each target's dependencies through the ports it doesn't build, and adds the targets reached as what the target needs first. So the order, `--only`'s added prerequisites, and a blocked dependent all follow those edges. Where the index can't be read, the plan orders by direct dependencies alone, as before, and `-v` says so.
- **Not changed, and said**: update already compares the updated port's own upstream requirements with what MacPorts has of each dependency, "requires X >=N, which MacPorts' X M doesn't meet", and holds where it isn't met, as review does. transformers 5.18.0 (a5ee76d0ec9) was merged without dockhand, so nothing ran the check on it. `logs --port` prints the port's log itself, as D20 decided, not its path.
