On 2026-10-23, field testing wrote its [reflections after some 300 bumps](../reviews/2026-10-23-field-reflections.md), and the person sent them to the architecture thread, ruling: "we won't consider the scope increase items, except for hold policies - that makes sense for current consideration alongside everything else" (20:10Z). The architecture thread [weighed them](../reviews/2026-10-23-field-reflections-architecture.md) at main aab5bafa, and they were coalesced into the roadmap the same day, after v0.5.0, since its acceptance is running. Each placement was read against the code at aab5bafa. The change is docs only.
