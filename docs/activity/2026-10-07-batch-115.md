# 2026-10-07: tart list bounded, and D-R1's disk images detached (batch 115)

From the rc8 full stage. Between 21:13 and 21:35Z status waited on `tart list --format json`, which asks diskutil about each VM's disk: two of those hung, on disks diskimagesiod had left attached and not mounted, and status, and the runner's isolate step with it, hung behind them. Not for rc8, whose run goes on.

## What changed

- **Every tart list is bounded** (`tart.Client.Images`): two minutes, where a listing takes about 30 seconds on the M1. One Tart doesn't answer in time is `tart.ErrListingTimedOut`, "Tart didn't answer tart list in 2m0s, so what VMs there are is unknown; a disk image Tart's listing waits on may be stuck attached (hdiutil info)". status, check, serve, setup, and clean all list through it. In status, check.on's resolution is the listing, and a failed one is the evidence's problem (batch 111's L5), so status goes on and says it.
- **status resolves check.on once a minute, not once a branch** (`requiredEnvironments`): each branch's evidence asked again, half a minute of Tart listing each, and two minutes each with a hung disk. The resolution stands for a minute within an engine, for the same check.on.
- **The harness bounds its own listing** (`harm_running`, 150 seconds).
- **D-R1's low-disk image is detached.** The row made it in `$(...)`, whose `FAULT_LOW_DISK` went with it, so the image was neither filled nor detached, and rc6's and rc8's were left attached in their results. `fault_low_disk_fill` and `_stop` take the row's own mount point where it's unset, `_stop` detaches by the image's path too, forced where it must, and D-R1's teardown calls it. The harm sweep lists any acceptance disk image still attached, a low-disk one or one under the stage's state, so H7 names one a row leaves, and `full.sh --end` says those left, to detach.

## Not changed

Tart has no cheaper documented call that says which images there are and which run without its disk queries, and reading its home's files would depend on what Tart doesn't document, so status still lists, bounded and once a minute.
