# A test's wait is bounded by the suite's settle

2026-10-27, first after rc6's tag (roadmap 4697148f). rc6's gate saw
`TestALocalCheckLeftUnfinishedIsPutBack` time out once beside the full
suite, then pass three times alone: its wait for the stopped check to
install libharbor was bounded at ten seconds, while that check starts
MacPorts' tclsh, runs the guest program, and snapshots the prefix.

- The settle, two minutes, moves to testsupport (`testsupport.Settle`),
  and cmdtest's `Settle` and the engine tests' `settleWait` name it.
- Every `Eventually` outside the command package that waits on a process
  or another goroutine takes it in place of its seconds: local's, the
  guest's two, script's two, coord's two, the runner's, the PortIndex
  cache's, https's, progress's, provision's, and git's branch lock. Each
  still ends as soon as its condition holds; none asserted a time, so
  none keeps its own bound. https's check that the rest wait their turn
  is its sleep after the wait, unchanged.

`make test`.
