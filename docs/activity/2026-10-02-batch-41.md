# 2026-10-02: field testing's wrong answers (batch 41)

Batch 41 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md), at the person's word: the bugs in field testing's command-surface report (dockhand at 7d244a01) that gave a wrong answer, ahead of the command-line UX review's queue, which waits on its discussion.

## What changed

- **rebase refuses a branch with nothing to rebase.** skim-egsi's pull request, #35103, had merged; `rebase` replayed nothing onto master, moved the branch there, and said "#35103 still has the old commits; dockhand submit replaces them" with "Next: dockhand check". Then `clean` kept the branch, now at master, as having commits beyond the merge. Rebase reads an open branch's pull request first, as `status --refresh` does, and refuses one merged ("dockhand clean … removes what it leaves") or closed ("dockhand archive … sets it aside"); and where master already has every change the branch makes, by a merge the record hasn't seen, it refuses before anything moves.
- **revbump of a revision python subports share.** `revbump py-lmdb` and `revbump py313-lmdb` were refused, "select a single literal subport block for py314-lmdb": the python PortGroup's subports have no block of their own, and take the Portfile's revision. `portfile.BumpRevision` bumps that line for a subport with no literal block, where its literal is the subport's revision; and the revision fidelity check lets a sibling at the selected port's revision rise with it, which is what "a revision shared by several subports is bumped for all of them" in revbump's help promised. A sibling with its own revision still may not move.
- **A local patch in a subdirectory of files.** review of perl5 (#35076) said "remote or ambiguous patchfile 5.16/clean-up-paths.patch" of every subport, of a file under files/5.16/. A patchfile is local where each of its path's segments is a plain name, so it stays under files; one with a site tag, `name:tag`, or out of files isn't, and is said in plain words.
- **A new port's dependencies.** review of py-mlx-vlm (#35088), a new port, said "requirements.txt adds X" of each requirement, holding, and its requirements no dependency is named for, pillow and tqdm among them, holding nothing. For a new port every requirement is new: the adds aren't said, and a requirement no dependency is named for holds. Names can differ, as py313-yaml for PyYAML, which is why an update's still doesn't.
- **What subports say alike is said once.** The same block repeated for each pyXY subport; submit's and review's comparisons group ports whose findings and coverage read the same, "py313-mlx-vlm, py314-mlx-vlm: …".
