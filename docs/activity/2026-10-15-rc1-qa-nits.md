# Two nits from v0.3.1-rc1's quick stage (v0.4.0)

The v0.3.1-rc1 quick stage on the M1 passed, and found two pieces of
wording that contradict what's done (Prime-time's write-up, the project's
plan/quick-run-2026-10-15-v0.3.1-rc1.md). Both are fixed on main, for
v0.4.0.

- **An obsolete stub the edit moved is said as moved, with or without
  `--with-obsolete`.** `update terraform-1.16 --new --plan` said the
  obsolete stub "stays at 1.16.0; --with-obsolete moves it", while its diff
  moved it to 1.16.6: terraform's stub's version line sits inside the main
  port's `if {${subport} eq ${name}}`, and moves with the shared release.
  The check that the edit moved it already (5eb101a3's) ran only behind
  `--with-obsolete`; it now runs first, so the preview says "Its obsolete
  terraform moves to 1.16.6 too, in the same commit."
- **The prune line counts only what was removed.** "removed what branches
  ended past 1 day recorded of 0 checks: 1 result and 0 provider runs…"
  read as a contradiction; it now reads "removed 1 result that branches
  ended past 1 day had recorded, keeping what reuse may choose and each
  branch's newest check".
