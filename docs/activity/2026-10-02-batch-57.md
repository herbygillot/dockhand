# 2026-10-02: kept archives signed once, in Go (batch 57)

Batch 57 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md): the library survey's archive signing, with the person's D18, "mapping only, no threshold", and its "yes" to signing once.

## What changed

- **The RSA signature is made in Go**, where `/usr/bin/openssl dgst -ripemd160 -sign` made it for every archive given to every guest. Go's `crypto.RIPEMD160` writes ISO's OID in the signed DigestInfo, which `openssl dgst -verify`, and so MacPorts, rejects; the signature is made over OpenSSL's own prefix, TeleTrusT's OID with NULL parameters, with `rsa.SignPKCS1v15` given no hash. The test signs with both and compares: byte for byte the same as this Mac's `openssl`, which the survey, on Linux with OpenSSL 3, couldn't try, and verified by it.
- **The archive is read through a read-only map** (`unix.Mmap`), for its digest and both signatures, whatever its size: the pages are the file's, which the system can drop, where the whole archive was read into memory. An empty archive, which can't be mapped, is read as empty. limits.md's gap is closed.
- **Each archive is signed once**: its `.sig` and `.rmd160` are kept beside it in the archive directory, named by a digest of the keys that made them, and uploaded from there to each guest, where they were made in the check's directory and removed after. The archive's digest is still checked every time it's given out. Cleanup removes them with their archive, and keeps them while it stays, whatever their age.
