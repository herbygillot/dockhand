# 2026-10-04: a large look says what it spends of GitHub's allowance (batch 87)

Batch 87 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md): from the M1's measurements at 1da4fdbf, which confirmed batch 86's reading: A10's own commands sent 42 requests in all, one `outdated --mine` over @herbygillot's 835 ports sent 2,166 in 267 seconds, about 2.6 a port, and no `gh` ran.

## What changed

- **A look over 300 ports or more says once what it will spend**, before it does: "Looking up 835 ports asks GitHub about 2,200 times, of the 5,000 an hour a login has." A maintainer with hundreds of ports, whom setup's `Next:` points at `outdated --mine`, learns the cost from the command itself. `prose.Count` sets thousands apart.
- **Spending less is under the roadmap's Later**: conditional requests with kept ETags, whose 304s GitHub doesn't count, and GraphQL batching of tag and release lookups.
