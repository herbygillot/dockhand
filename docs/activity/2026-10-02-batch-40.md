# 2026-10-02: a change to one subport is that subport's (batch 40)

Batch 40 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md), from the field-testing thread's terraform-1.16 bump (1.16.3 to 1.16.5, macports/macports-ports#35104, dockhand at 7d244a01), taken next at the person's word. A Portfile's scope is its directory, named for its main port, so an edit inside one subport's block read as changing terraform: the commit rules held tidy's own subject, submit's upstream section listed eleven ports for one, and the search for other pull requests looked under terraform.

## What changed

- **A subject may name a subport its directory defines.** The commit rules took the directory's name alone, so "terraform-1.16: update to 1.16.5", which tidy wrote and master's history uses, was held as `subject-port` and the bump exited 3: tidy and the rules disagreed. Each commit now carries the ports its directories define, as base's port index names them (`portsDefined`, which impact and review already read), for tidy, submit, and review alike. A name the directory doesn't define still holds.
- **A subport whose source didn't change says nothing of upstream.** `macports.SameSource` compares what a port fetches and patches, its version, epoch, sites, distfiles, checksums, Git tag, source directory, and patch files, whatever its revision. A revision's assessment of such a port records `source-unchanged` and compares nothing; submit's and review's comparisons leave it out, where terraform's update said "Compared LICENSE.txt" of nine unchanged subports and "fetches no upstream source" of two more.
- **Other pull requests are searched for under the ports whose source changed**, terraform-1.16, rather than the directory's terraform; under the directories' names where none did, as a revbump. Submit now searches after it assesses.
- **Ports in a person's order.** `macports.ComparePortNames` orders a run of digits by its number, terraform-1.2 before terraform-1.10; a directory's subports, and so a check's targets and Tested on, and the comparisons, are ordered by it.

## Not changed

- **A check builds every subport of a changed Portfile**, all 18 of terraform's. MacPorts' CI does the same: `.github/workflows/main.yml` lists the changed directories and builds `mpbb list-subports` of them, every subport. Building only terraform-1.16 would claim less than the pull request's CI will build, so check stays CI's equal. A person who wants fewer builds has `--only`.
- **New subports** aren't in base's index, so a subject naming one added by the branch still reads as not naming its port; the directory's name passes.

## Verification

- Tests: `TestASubjectMayNameTheSubportItChanges`, `TestASubportWhoseSourceDidntChangeIsLeftOut`, and `TestPortNamesAreOrderedAsAPersonReadsThem`.
- The full suite with `DOCKHAND_TEST_MACPORTS_TCLSH`; fmt-check, vendor-check, vet, deadcode, and lint, each read for its exit status.
