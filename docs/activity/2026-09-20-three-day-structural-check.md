# Three days measured, and what they earned

Asked whether the last three days had made the architecture or the code organization worse, the answer was measured rather than remembered. Sixty-one commits since `0dfbcad` on the 17th. Excluding vendor: 4,021 non-test lines added and 1,356 removed across 135 files, and 3,472 test lines added, which moved the test-to-code ratio from 0.70 to 0.73. Internal import edges went from 238 to 245 with none removed. Files over 400 lines went from seven to eight, the eighth being the expression parser. The two dependency-contract tests were untouched and pass. Vendoring dwarfs all of it by volume, by decision, and makes every `log --stat` noisier from here on.

Of the seven new edges, five were expected: `app` wiring a provider at the composition root, `macports/eval` taking spans, `verify/github` reporting progress as `verify/tart` already did, and both providers stamping the build on evidence, which is the price of `verify`'s import contract. Two were not, and both had slipped in because `cli` had no contract test of the kind `verify` and `assess` have. `cli` imported `verify/tart` for one constant in a flag's help text; the effective timeout now reaches it as `app.Config.TestTimeout`, which is also the value the help should name. `upstream` imported `macports/dependency` for one sentinel, discovery depending on the editing side for an error value; `ErrManifestMissing` lives in `macports` now, the facts package both already import, and `dependency` aliases it so its own references and its callers' are unchanged. `cli` has its allowlist test, which checks this module's packages and not the third-party ones a command layer legitimately uses.

Two files had grown a third or more without changing shape. `workflow/view/contribution.go`, the largest file in the tree at 692 lines, held the projection that folds jobs into contributions and the phrasebook that words them; the phrasebook is its own file. `portedit/unmodeled.go` held the read classification and the explanation of host accesses by their frames; the explanation is `host_access.go`. Neither split changes a signature or a test. `workflow/request.go` grew 49 percent but as rules in its action table, whose largest function is 43 lines, and was left alone.

Three warts were recorded as accepted rather than fixed, each with its reason where it was made: `RefreshBody` rides on the publication destination, a how on a where, because the alternative threaded a parameter through five signatures; the build is stamped on evidence by each provider rather than once, to keep `verify`'s contract; and a braced word of an ordinary command is still walked as a possible script, since a subport's or a variant's body is exactly that and nothing structural tells it from text.
